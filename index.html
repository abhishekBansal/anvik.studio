Principles

        
What every app promises you.

      

      

        

          01
          
Private by design

          
Your data stays on your device. We don't add telemetry or accounts, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

        

        

          02
          
Fast by default

          
Apps open instantly and stay light. We avoid dependencies we don't need, so each one stays quick on modest hardware.

        

        

          03
          
Focused on purpose

          
Each app solves one problem well. We avoid feature creep, lock-in and subscription traps.
