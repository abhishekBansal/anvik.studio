Privacy Policy

Active Policy

For the mobile application Dainik Panchang (दैनिक पंचांग) developed by Anvik Studio.

Application Dainik Panchang
Package ID studio.anvik.dainikpanchang
Last Updated November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

At Anvik Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), we hold your personal privacy as a fundamental engineering principle. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you download, install, and use the Dainik Panchang (दैनिक पंचांग) mobile application (the "App") distributed via the Google Play Store.

Dainik Panchang is designed with an offline-first, privacy-by-default architecture. It is built to offer seniors and families a calm, high-contrast, uncluttered Hindu Panchang calendar and festival guide without unnecessary tracking, invasive data collection, or mandatory account logins.

2. Information Collection & Storage

A. Personal Information

We do not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). The App does not require registration, login, phone number, email address, contacts, camera, or file access.

B. Local On-Device Preferences

Any preferences you customize inside the App—such as your selected district/city (for local sunrise, sunset, and tithi calculation), fasting tradition preference (Smartha or Vaishnava), high-contrast display toggle, and haptic feedback toggle—are stored strictly locally on your device using Android’s secure internal storage (SharedPreferences / DataStore).

These preferences are never synced, transmitted, or backed up to any server operated by Anvik Studio.

C. Location Data

The App does not request or use runtime GPS or network-based precise location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION). You select your city or district manually from our preloaded offline catalog of 788+ Indian districts.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Service Providers

Future Provision

Current Status: Dainik Panchang is currently 100% ad-free and contains no embedded advertising SDKs.

Future Advertising Disclosure: To keep Dainik Panchang freely accessible and maintain ongoing updates, compatibility releases, and server maintenance, future versions of the App may integrate reputable third-party advertising networks, such as Google AdMob (Google Play Services).

If and when advertising services are enabled:

  • Third-party ad networks may collect and process pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP addresses, mobile carrier, operating system version, and general diagnostic signals to deliver, personalize, and measure advertisements.
  • These partners use these signals to combat ad fraud, ensure ad frequency capping, and present non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with Google Play Developer policies and applicable global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).
  • We do not share any personal name, phone number, or private app settings with advertising providers.

Third-Party Privacy Practices:

Third-party partners are governed by their own respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob & Ad Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Your Choices & Opt-Out Controls

You have full control over advertising identifiers and personalized ads on your Android device:

How to manage or reset your Advertising ID:

  1. Open your device’s Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  4. You can also enable Opt out of Ads Personalization to prevent advertisers from serving interest-based ads.

5. Device Permissions

Dainik Panchang utilizes only the minimal permissions required for its functionality:

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+)

Used exclusively to show scheduled daily tithi alerts or vrat reminders that you configure. All reminders are generated entirely locally on your device.

VIBRATE

Used to provide tactile haptic feedback on button presses for improved accessibility and senior-friendly usability.

INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Reserved for future advertising delivery and connectivity checks. The core Panchang calculations and calendar operate 100% offline.

6. Children's Privacy

Dainik Panchang is a general-audience cultural utility and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of majority in your jurisdiction). If we become aware that personal information of a child has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete that information.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Because we do not maintain a cloud account or backend database for your app usage, you retain complete ownership of your data at all times.

You can erase all locally stored data instantly at any time by clearing the app’s storage via Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Dainik Panchang → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the App from your device.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new capabilities, or legal requirements. When updates are published, the revised date at the top of this page will be updated. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
